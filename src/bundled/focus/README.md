# Focus

Work with your agents without the rest of Buzz competing for attention.

## Getting started

1. Open the menu under your profile picture and choose **Focus**.
2. Pick how long, from 30 minutes to the rest of today or a custom end time,
   then choose **Start focus**.
3. The sidebar now shows only your Solo chats, and only they can show banners,
   play sounds or light the Dock dot. Your status becomes 🔭 Focusing.
4. Focus ends by itself, or choose **End** in the sidebar. Your tabs, alerts
   and earlier status come back.

---

## Details

- Focus is saved on this device and survives a restart. The status is set in
  the selected community only, and is restored only if it is still 🔭.
- Alerts from everything else, including Reminders, are skipped rather than
  replayed later; other conversations keep their unread state.
- Turning the plugin off ends the Solo-only sidebar and alerts at once; the 🔭
  status still clears itself at the end time.
