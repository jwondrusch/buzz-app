# Focus

Work with your agents without the rest of Buzz competing for attention.

## Getting started

1. Open the menu under your profile picture and choose **Focus**.
2. Pick how long, from 30 minutes to the rest of today or a custom end time,
   then choose **Start focus**. Or press ⌘⇧F (Ctrl+Shift+F) to start Focus
   for the last length you chose, and again to end it.
3. The sidebar, search and links now reach only your Solo chats, and only they
   can show banners, play sounds or light the Dock dot. Your status becomes
   🔭 Focusing.
4. Other alerts are saved on the **Focus alerts** page. Open it from the
   Focus dialog, from search, or from the notification when Focus ends.
5. Focus ends by itself, or choose **End** in the sidebar. Your tabs, alerts
   and earlier status come back.

---

## Details

- Focus is saved on this device and survives a restart. The status is set in
  the selected community only, and is restored only if it is still 🔭.
- Alerts from everything else, including Reminders, are saved on this device
  for the account they arrived for, newest 200, until you open or clear them.
  They open once Focus ends. Other conversations keep their unread state.
- If Focus ended while Buzz was closed, the summary comes once you reconnect.
- Turning the plugin off ends Focus and restores your earlier status. Quitting
  Buzz keeps Focus on for next time.
